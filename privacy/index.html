Privacy & disclosures

Affiliate links

Some outbound links (for example to ActivityHero) may be affiliate links. If you book through them we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. They are marked “affiliate”.

Featured listings

Camps can pay for a featured placement. Featured cards are always labeled and their facts follow the same sourcing rules.

No endorsements or safety vetting

We have not inspected, licensed, or safety-vetted any program. Confirm licensing, staff screening and policies directly with the provider.

Email sign-up alerts

If you ask to be emailed when a camp or activity opens sign-ups, we collect your email address plus the camp, activity categories, season(s) and optional area you picked. That information is submitted to our signup endpoint (a Cloudflare Worker) and stored with our email provider (Resend) so we can send the alerts you asked for, and occasional related reminders such as deadlines for the same camps. Every alert email includes a one-click unsubscribe link and a link to change your area and category preferences. You can also ask us to delete your data anytime. We never sell or share your email with camps or advertisers.

Data we collect

Apart from alert sign-ups, we do not collect personal data. We do not sell personal data. The site sets no cookies of its own. We count page views and outbound clicks with a cookieless beacon (site, path, referrer host, country, coarse device class, and a daily salted visitor hash — never a raw IP). Known bots and our own internal test browsers are excluded. Our email provider may use its own cookies or tracking on its sign-up confirmation pages and emails under its own privacy policy.
