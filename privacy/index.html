Privacy & disclosures

Affiliate links

Some outbound links (for example to ActivityHero) may be affiliate links. If you book through them we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. They are marked “affiliate”.

Featured listings

Camps can pay for a featured placement. Featured cards are always labeled and their facts follow the same sourcing rules.

No endorsements or safety vetting

We have not inspected, licensed, or safety-vetted any program. Confirm licensing, staff screening and policies directly with the provider.

Email sign-up alerts

If you ask to be emailed when a camp or activity opens sign-ups, we collect your email address plus the camp, activity categories, season(s) and optional area you picked. That information is submitted to our signup endpoint (a Cloudflare Worker) and stored with our email provider (Resend) so we can send the alerts you asked for, and occasional related reminders such as deadlines for the same camps. Every alert email includes a one-click unsubscribe link and a personal, signed link to change your area and category preferences; changes to an existing sign-up need that link or a signed-in account, so nobody else can change or cancel your alerts. Alert emails are reviewed by a person before they go out. You can also ask us to delete your data anytime. We never sell or share your email with camps or advertisers.

Optional accounts (My camp calendar)

Accounts are free and optional; the directory, Summer Planner and alert sign-up all work without one. There is no password: when you ask to sign in, we email a one-time link to the address you typed (it expires after 15 minutes). That sign-in email is the only email an account sends unless you turn on alerts.

If you create an account we store: your email address; the camps you save and the alert switches you set for each one; a copy of your Summer Planner (if you use it while signed in); whether you paused emails; when the account was created and last signed in; a private calendar-feed code; and your sign-ins, saved only as one-way hashes. To limit abuse we also keep a hashed form of the address for 24 hours each time a sign-in link is requested. This lives in a Cloudflare D1 database used only by our signup Worker. Your alert choices and email address are kept with our email provider (Resend) when you turn on any alert. Your browser keeps your sign-in in local storage (akc_session), not a cookie.

Your private calendar feed link shows your saved camps to anyone who has it, so share it only with family. Calendar entries use only dates the camps have published; anything else is shown as “not announced yet”.

Pause all emails on your account page stops every alert and reminder right away. Delete my account immediately and permanently removes your account, saved camps, planner copy and sign-ins from our database and deletes your contact from our email provider, then shows a confirmation. Your planner may still be saved in your own browser until you clear it.

Data we collect

Apart from alert sign-ups and optional accounts (above), we do not collect personal data. We do not sell personal data. The site sets no cookies of its own. We count page views and outbound clicks with a cookieless beacon (site, path, referrer host, country, coarse device class, and a daily salted visitor hash — never a raw IP). Known bots and our own internal test browsers are excluded. Our email provider may use its own cookies or tracking on its sign-up confirmation pages and emails under its own privacy policy.
