Provider
SailATL (at Atlanta Yacht Club)
Where
Acworth, on Lake Allatoona (Atlanta Yacht Club) · Cobb
Ages
Saturday program: 6 years and up (must be 6 by September 1; parent or guardian must stay on site). Weekday sail camp: 9 years and up (must be 9 by September 1). All participants must be able to swim 25 yards; a swim test is given the first day. (ages 6–18 for filters)
Price
$400 AYC members / $550 non-members per weekday week; Saturday program $175 members / $400 non-members
Official 2027 fees on the provider page (checked 2026-11-23): weekday camp AYC member $400 / non-member $550 per week; Saturday program AYC member $175 / non-member $400. Hours: weekday 9:30am–4:00pm; Saturday 9:00am–12:00pm. The page still shows leftover “Class is Full” labels next to the 2027 weekday weeks; the official 2027 status is “Registration will open soon,” so those weeks are not recorded as full.
